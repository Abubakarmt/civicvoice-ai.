Civic Education

Voter Registration

Eligible citizens must complete voter registration with INEC. Turning 18 does not automatically register a person.

INEC

INEC is Nigeria's independent electoral body, with responsibilities established by the Constitution and electoral laws.

BVAS

BVAS means Bimodal Voter Accreditation System. It is used in voter accreditation and related electoral processes.

Peaceful Participation

Respect other citizens, reject electoral violence and consult reliable information.

Sources and Transparency
